AttentionView — Social & Search Market Intelligence Platform

AttentionView is a real-time market intelligence platform that lets users analyze global attention, demand, and trends across Google, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook — the same way traders analyze financial markets on TradingView. The platform tracks what people are searching, watching, engaging with, and buying across all major digital platforms, giving businesses, marketers, and decision-makers the data they need to make informed market entry, product, and campaign decisions.

Core Features

Attention Index — a scored, charted metric per topic or keyword across all platforms, with timeframes (1D, 1W, 1M, 3M, 1Y), few trending nitches,product or topics under each platform.

Top Searches — ranked global search volumes by topic, niche, or keyword with platform source and percentage change

Trending Products — physical and digital products gaining search and engagement momentum globally

Breakout Keywords — emerging keywords before they peak, scored and ranked as early-entry signals

Niche Rankings — broad market categories ranked by growth velocity across all platforms

Regional Interest — country-level breakdown showing where demand for any topic is rising or falling

Market Signals — breakout, rising, declining, watch, and fading alerts per topic

Platform Breakdown — data segmented per platform so users can see where attention lives

Live Activity Feed — real-time updates per platform as trends emerge

Watchlist — users track selected topics and monitor their index scores over time

Screener — filter all topics by platform, region, growth rate, and category

Compare View — side-by-side comparison of two or more topics across platforms and regions

Target Users

Businesses deciding where and what to sell, marketers planning campaigns, entrepreneurs spotting untapped niches, and anyone making decisions based on where global demand is moving.

Include all necessary data neede for users to analyze and make decisons
